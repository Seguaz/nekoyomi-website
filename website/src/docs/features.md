# Features

**Nekoyomi** keeps everything [Aniyomi](https://github.com/aniyomiorg/aniyomi) does and adds a lot on top of it.
These are the highlights, each with a link to where it's explained.
For every change, version by version, see the [changelogs](/changelogs/).

## Light novels

- [Light novels](/docs/guides/novels): a third content type, with its own library, categories, updates and history.
- [Text reader](/docs/guides/novels#reading): adjustable font, size, spacing and margins, and auto-scroll that continues into the next chapter.
- [Local novels](/docs/guides/novels#local-novels): read your own `.epub` files.

## Library

- [Custom series](/docs/faq/library#how-do-i-group-several-entries-into-one-series): group several entries into one folder with its own cover.
- [Group by](/docs/faq/library#how-do-i-group-my-library-by-source-status-or-tag): source, status, language, tag or tracking status, without touching your categories.
- [Tag filter](/docs/faq/library#how-do-i-filter-my-library-by-tags): show or hide entries by tag.
- [Pin to top](/docs/faq/library#how-do-i-keep-entries-at-the-top-of-a-category): keep your favorites above everything else.
- [Category options](/docs/guides/categories#category-options): private categories and a default reading mode per category.
- [Watch and read counter](/docs/faq/library#how-do-i-see-how-many-times-i-ve-watched-or-read-something): see how many times you've finished each episode or chapter, and sort by it.

## Player and reader

- [Hold to speed up](/docs/guides/video-player/#long-press): hold for 2x speed and slide to change it.
- [Online subtitles](/docs/guides/video-player/sheets#search-opensubtitles): search OpenSubtitles without leaving the player.
- [Automatic source fallback](/docs/guides/player-settings/internal-player-settings#automatic-source-fallback) *(experimental)*: switch to another source when one has no video.
- [Reduce screen tearing](/docs/guides/player-settings/decoder#reduce-screen-tearing) *(experimental)*.
- [Webtoon auto-scroll](/docs/guides/reader-settings#auto-scroll): hands-free reading with adjustable speed.

## Browse and migration

- [Mass migration](/docs/guides/source-migration#migrating-several-series-at-once): migrate many series in one go.
- [Source priority](/docs/guides/source-migration#source-priority): choose which sources are searched first.
- [Newer manga extensions](/docs/faq/browse/extensions#which-repositories-can-i-add): support for the tachiyomix 1.6 format, used by repositories such as Keiyoushi.
- [Import from the app](/docs/guides/local-manga-source/#importing-from-the-app): add files to your local sources without a file manager.

## Look and feel

- [Navigation bar](/docs/faq/settings#how-do-i-customize-the-navigation-bar): choose and reorder your tabs, and use a floating bar with adjustable opacity, blur and height.
- [App icons](/docs/faq/settings#how-do-i-change-the-app-icon): including icons designed by the community.
- [Cover backdrop](/docs/faq/settings#how-do-i-change-the-blurred-cover-behind-entries): each entry shows its cover as a blurred banner behind the header.

## Updates and downloads

- [Update at a specific time](/docs/faq/library#can-i-choose-what-time-the-library-updates) of day.
- [Downloads that resume on their own](/docs/faq/downloads#why-did-my-downloads-stop-midway) after losing connection.
- [Updates list widget](/docs/faq/general#does-nekoyomi-have-home-screen-widgets) for your home screen.
- [Hide the source name](/docs/faq/settings#can-i-hide-which-source-an-entry-comes-from) when sharing screenshots.
