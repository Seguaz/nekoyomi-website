# Privacy policy

Nekoyomi is a free, open-source app.
It is provided at no cost and is intended for use as is.

This page explains what data the **Nekoyomi app** and the **Nekoyomi website** handle.
The short version: we don't collect anything about you.

## The app {#the-app}

### No analytics, no tracking, no accounts {#no-analytics-no-tracking-no-accounts}

The app contains **no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting services**.
There is no Nekoyomi account, and we don't run any server that receives your data.

Your library, history, categories, settings and backups are stored **only on your device** (and wherever you choose to save your backups).

### Crash logs {#crash-logs}

If the app crashes, nothing is sent anywhere automatically.
You can create a crash log yourself from <nav to="advanced"> and decide whether to share it, for example when reporting a bug.
Review it before sharing, as it may include details about your device and app configuration.

### Network connections made by the app {#network-connections-made-by-the-app}

The app only connects to the internet to do what you ask it to:

- **Sources and extensions** you install, and the **extension repositories** you add, to browse, read, watch and download content.
  These are third-party services that are not operated by us.
- **Trackers** (e.g. MyAnimeList, AniList) and other optional services, only if you log in to or enable them.
- **OpenSubtitles**, only when you search for subtitles from the video player. The search sends the title, the season and episode number when known, and the subtitle languages, without any account.
- **GitHub**, to check for app updates and to download them.

Each of these services has its own privacy policy, which we recommend you review.

## This website {#this-website}

This website is a static site hosted on **GitHub Pages**.

- It uses **no cookies, no analytics and no third-party scripts**.
- Your light/dark theme choice is stored only in your own browser.
- Like any web host, GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address to deliver the site and keep it secure.
  See [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## External links {#external-links}

Both the app and this website contain links to other sites that are not operated by us.
We have no control over and assume no responsibility for their content, privacy policies, or practices.

## Changes to this privacy policy {#changes-to-this-privacy-policy}

We may update this privacy policy from time to time.
Any changes will be published on this page.

The current version of this policy is effective as of November 19, 2026.
